## NAATI Certified Interpreter (CI)
Since 2023 the CI credential = CPI test + CI test. **The CI test has no dialogue:** sight translation both ways (~200 words, 5 + 7 min), consecutive monologue both ways (~300 words, 3 segments), simultaneous monologue both ways (~300 words). Online, 5-band rubric, Band 2+ on every criterion in all six tasks. Dialogue practice prepares people for CPI, not CI — so no CI landing page (it would mislead).
Sources: naati.com.au/certification/ci/, /resources/candidate-instructions-ci/, CI and CPI assessment rubrics (PDF).

## OET Speaking
Two ~5-min role-plays, 3 min prep each, candidate plays own profession and normally opens; interlocutor plays patient/relative. Profession-specific (12). Criteria: 4 linguistic (0–6: intelligibility, fluency, appropriateness, grammar & expression) + 5 clinical communication (0–3). Score 0–500; grades A 450+, B 350–440, C+ 300–340. **AHPRA from 23 Apr 2026: Speaking 360** (earlier sittings: B). OET@Home not accepted by AHPRA.
Sources: oet.com/test-information/speaking/, oet.com speaking criteria, ahpra.gov.au accepted English tests.

## IELTS Speaking
11–14 min, examiner-led: Part 1 (4–5 min), Part 2 cue card (1 min prep, 1–2 min talk), Part 3 discussion (4–5 min). Four equal criteria: fluency & coherence, lexical resource, grammatical range & accuracy, pronunciation. Pronunciation can't be judged from a transcript → XINGO gives an estimated band from the other criteria and says so.
Sources: ielts.org speaking format, IELTS speaking band descriptors (PDF).

## AMC Clinical Examination
IMGs (standard pathway) after the CAT MCQ; alternative is WBA. 16 assessed stations, 2 min reading + 8 min; history, examination, diagnostic formulation, management/counselling. Key steps + domain ratings + global rating (≥4/7 passes a station); pass ≥9 of 14 scored stations. Online format has no hands-on exam.
Sources: amc.org.au clinical exam, Clinical Exam Specifications V8 (Apr 2025).

## NMBA RN OSCE (OBA for IQNMs)
Stream B after NCLEX-RN. 10 stations, 2 min reading + 8 min, simulated patients/carers/colleagues, assessed against RN Standards for Practice; ISBAR named explicitly. Adelaide and Melbourne; AUD 4,000. Fast-track pathway since 2025 for some jurisdictions (UK, Ireland, US, Canada BC/ON, Singapore, Spain).
Sources: nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au OSCE & RN pages, RN OSCE Candidate Handbook (Jul 2026).

## NBCMI CMI oral
After written Hub-CMI. Oral in Spanish, Russian, Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean, Vietnamese. Sight translation (2 docs, into LOTE) then 12 mini-scenarios × 4 utterances consecutive both ways, 8 s think time, 6 repeats total. Criteria: accuracy, listening & retention, grammar, interpreting style, terminology. Pass 65–80% by language.
Sources: NBCMI Candidate Handbook (Feb 2026), oral exam prep document.

## CCHI CHI oral
After CoreCHI. CHI oral in Spanish, Mandarin, Arabic; others take CoreCHI-Performance (ETOE, English-only). 60 min: 4 consecutive vignettes (≈70% weight), 2 simultaneous, 3 sight, 1 MC. Scales: lexical accuracy, grammar, quality of speech; scaled 300–600, pass 450. Test centre only.
Sources: cchicertification.org CHI description & score pages.

## US state court oral (NCSC) — not built
Sight both ways (~225 words), consecutive attorney–witness 22–30 min (both directions, 2 whole-turn repeats), simultaneous monologue ~800 words. Scoring units; 70% per section. FCICE (federal, Spanish only) 80%. Next candidate: a "court consecutive" module with direct/cross examination scenarios.
Sources: NCSC Oral Exam Overview (rev. Nov 2023), FCICE Examinee Handbook (Nov 2025).

## UK DPSI — not built
CIOL Level 6. Now only Law and Health pathways (Sept 2025 spec). Unit 01: one ~20-min live consecutive role-play + two ~4-min simultaneous recordings. Units 02–05 sight/written translation. Marked 0–12 per outcome (accuracy; delivery; language).
Sources: CIOL DPSI qualification specification (Sept 2025), U01 sample paper.
